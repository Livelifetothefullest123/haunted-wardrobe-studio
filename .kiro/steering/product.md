# Product steering

Haunted Wardrobe Studio is a Halloween costume planner for girls and women.

- The user is choosing a costume for a real night out, not browsing a catalogue. Keep the flow short: four picks, one complete plan, no extra steps.
- Every plan must be complete and usable: what to wear, what to carry, hair and makeup, whether to DIY or buy, what it costs, and what to tick off before leaving.
- Spooky design must help, not hide: dark haunted colours, large buttons, high contrast, and a light plan card so the details stay readable.
- All costume themes are generic (witch, vampire, ghost, pumpkin, fairy, pirate, skeleton, cat). Never use copyrighted or trademarked characters, names, or franchises.
- Tone is warm, playful, and encouraging. No scary gore, no exclusionary language.
